Krylov-Safonov regularity theory

Main Elliptic Parabolic Kinetic Hypoelliptic Source material

We study the Krylov–Safonov theorem and related results across elliptic, parabolic, kinetic, and hypoelliptic PDEs.

Krylov–Safonov theory

The Krylov–Safonov theory establishes quantitative regularity for uniformly elliptic equations in nondivergence form with merely measurable coefficients. In the elliptic setting, write

\[ \begin{gathered} Lu=\operatorname{tr}\!\bigl(A(x)D^2u\bigr),\\ \lambda I\le A(x)\le\Lambda I,\\ 0<\lambda\le\Lambda. \end{gathered} \]

Theorem:

Every nonnegative suitable solution of \(Lu=0\) in \(B_{2r}\) satisfies

\[ \begin{gathered} \sup_{B_r}u\le C\inf_{B_r}u,\\ C=C(n,\Lambda/\lambda). \end{gathered} \]

Oscillation decay then gives, for solutions of either sign,

\[ [u]_{C^\alpha(B_r)} \le Cr^{-\alpha}\|u\|_{L^\infty(B_{2r})}, \]

where \(\alpha>0\) also depends only on dimension and ellipticity. The key is that these estimates require no continuity of \(A\) and no smallness of its ellipticity ratio. See Safonov (J. Soviet Math., 1983; Russian original 1980, DOI).

Historical contributions and the role of ABP

The key ingredient was the Aleksandrov–Bakelman–Pucci maximum estimate, developed through works of Aleksandrov (Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR, 1960, archive), Bakelman (Sibirsk. Mat. Zh., 1961, archive), and Pucci (Ann. Mat. Pura Appl., 1966, DOI). For \(Lu=f\) in a bounded domain \(\Omega\), with \(u=0\) on \(\partial\Omega\), its basic form is

\[ \|u\|_{L^\infty(\Omega)} \le \frac{C_n}{\lambda}\, \operatorname{diam}(\Omega)\, \|f\|_{L^n(\Omega)}. \]

Krylov–Safonov (Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR, 1979, archive) used a probabilistic method to establish quantitative propagation of positivity from sets of positive measure. Growth estimates and covering arguments convert this mechanism into Harnack’s inequality and Hölder continuity. The full parabolic result appeared in Krylov–Safonov (Math. USSR-Izvestiya, 1981; Russian original 1980, DOI), alongside the elliptic treatment of Safonov (J. Soviet Math., 1983; Russian original 1980, DOI).

This theory became a central ingredient in fully nonlinear elliptic regularity, notably the \(C^{2,\alpha}\) estimates for uniformly elliptic convex or concave equations established by Evans (Comm. Pure Appl. Math., 1982, DOI) and Krylov (Math. USSR-Izvestiya, 1983; Russian original 1982, DOI).

We explore Astra’s solution of the following previously open problems.

  • Kinetic Krylov–Safonov estimates for equations in nondivergence form with merely measurable coefficients (Silvestre, IMA Vol. Math. Appl., 2023, §8.2, DOI).
  • Harnack inequalities on the Heisenberg group for nondivergence operators with merely measurable coefficients and arbitrary ellipticity ratio (Tralli, Rend. Lincei Mat. Appl., 2012, DOI).
  • Pucci’s conjecture on the sharp \(L^p\)-to-\(L^\infty\) maximum estimate in dimensions \(n\ge3\) (Pucci, Ann. Mat. Pura Appl., 1966, DOI; Trudinger, Indiana Univ. Math. J., 2020, DOI).
