Krylov-Safonov regularity theory

Main Elliptic Parabolic Kinetic Hypoelliptic Source material

The Krylov–Safonov theory establishes quantitative regularity for elliptic PDEs in nondivergence form with merely measurable coefficients. Let \(A=(a_{ij})\) satisfy \(\lambda I\le A(x)\le\Lambda I\) for constants \(0<\lambda\le\Lambda\). We write

\[ Lu=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}a_{ij}(x)\,\partial_{ij}^{2}u. \]

Theorem: Every nonnegative suitable solution of \(Lu=0\) in \(B_{2r}\) satisfies

\[ \sup_{B_r}u\le C\inf_{B_r}u, \]

where \(C=C(n,\Lambda/\lambda)>0\). Moreover, every suitable solution of \(Lu=0\) in \(B_{2r}\) satisfies

\[ [u]_{\mathrm{C}^\alpha(B_r)} \le Cr^{-\alpha}\|u\|_{\mathrm{L}^\infty(B_{2r})}, \]

where \(\alpha=\alpha(d,\lambda,\Lambda)>0\).

These estimates require no continuity of \(A\) and no smallness of its ellipticity ratio. See Safonov (J. Soviet Math., 1983; Russian original 1980, DOI).

The role of ABP

The key ingredient was the Aleksandrov–Bakelman–Pucci estimate, developed in works of Aleksandrov (Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR, 1960, archive), Bakelman (Sibirsk. Mat. Zh., 1961, archive), and Pucci (Ann. Mat. Pura Appl., 1966, DOI). For \(Lu=f\) in a bounded domain \(\Omega\), with \(u=0\) on \(\partial\Omega\),

\[ \|u\|_{\mathrm{L}^\infty(\Omega)} \le \frac{C_n}{\lambda}\, \operatorname{diam}(\Omega)\, \|f\|_{\mathrm{L}^n(\Omega)}. \]

Krylov–Safonov (Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR, 1979, archive) used a probabilistic method to establish quantitative propagation of positivity from sets of positive measure. A growth lemma and a covering argument yield the Harnack inequality and Hölder continuity. The parabolic result appeared in Krylov–Safonov (Math. USSR-Izvestiya, 1981; Russian original 1980, DOI), alongside the elliptic treatment of Safonov (J. Soviet Math., 1983; Russian original 1980, DOI).
