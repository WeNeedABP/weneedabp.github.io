Krylov–Safonov theory

We study Krylov–Safonov theory for elliptic, parabolic, kinetic, and hypoelliptic partial differential equations.

This project is a pragmatic response to the increasing capabilities of AI, including its ability to prove very hard theorems autonomously. Our aim is to collect AI-generated results in this area alongside human digestions that explain, distil, and develop the ideas behind them.

We begin with a small collection and plan to expand it over time, bringing together new results, clearer explanations, and directions for future work.

The collection

AI results preserve the outputs and how they were obtained. Human digestions extract the mathematical ideas. Future work will gather the questions and directions that follow.
