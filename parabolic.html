An entropy approach to parabolic ABP

Astra’s proof of the kinetic Krylov–Safonov theorem is based on a novel approach to the ABP estimate. It derives an entropy estimate for the adjoint equation by regularising along an auxiliary flow and exploiting entropy dissipation.

In this exposition, we explain the proof in the parabolic case.
