Initial prompt

Generalize the adjoint ABP estimate in kinetic ABP-adjoint.tex from the kinetic group to a general stratified Carnot group, focusing on the actual proof mechanism: the smoothing kernel K_h, the identities behind \partial_tQ and \partial_hQ, the auxiliary energy F, and the key differential inequality yielding the adjoint L^q bound and then ABP by duality, and identify what replaces the kinetic Gaussian. Give a rigorous theorem for the ABP for general Carnot group
